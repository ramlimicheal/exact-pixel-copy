Privacy policy

Last Updated: 24.02.2026


At Enra, we respect your privacy and are committed to protecting your personal data.


This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, and safeguard information when you visit our website enra.framer.media.

1. Information We Collect

  • Personal Data: Name, email, phone when you contact us or subscribe.

  • Usage Data: IP address, browser type, pages visited (via cookies/analytics).

  • No Sensitive Data: We do not collect financial info or health data.

2. How We Use Your Data

  • To respond to inquiries and provide services.

  • Improve site experience and send updates (with opt-out).

  • Analytics for better performance.

3. Cookies & Tracking

We use essential cookies for functionality and analytics (Google Analytics). Manage preferences via browser settings.

4. Sharing Your Data

We do not sell data. Shared only with service providers (e.g., hosting, email tools) under strict agreements. Complies with GDPR/CCPA.

5. Your Rights

Access, correct, delete, or export your data. Contact us at hello@enra.com.

6. Security & Changes

Data is secured with encryption. We may update this policy—check back regularly.


Questions? Contact us at hello@enra.com.
