Urban Kicks

A high-energy visual identity and e-commerce store for a streetwear sneaker marketplace.

The Challenge

Generic Templates

Urban Kicks was running on a basic Shopify theme that couldn't handle their "Drop Culture" traffic spikes and lacked the edgy, street-style aesthetic of their products.

Our Solution

Immersive 3D & Headless Commerce

We moved them to a headless CMS with a Framer front-end. We introduced 3D product rotations on the homepage and a countdown-timer "Drop Zone" that creates hype without crashing the site.

The Results
